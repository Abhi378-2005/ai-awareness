Five habits for using AI responsibly

None of this requires being an expert — just a little healthy skepticism.

Verify before you trust

AI tools can sound confident while being wrong. Cross-check facts, dates, and citations before relying on them.

Know what data you're sharing

Avoid pasting personal, medical, or confidential information into AI tools you don't fully trust.

Watch for bias in the output

If a result seems to favor one group, viewpoint, or assumption unfairly, question where that pattern came from.

Keep a human in the loop

For anything important — medical, legal, financial, academic — treat AI output as a draft, not a final answer.

Stay a little curious

Understanding roughly how a tool works is the best defense against being misled by it.
