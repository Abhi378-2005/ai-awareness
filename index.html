What is AI, really?

No jargon — just the idea underneath it.

Artificial Intelligence is software that learns patterns from large amounts of data, then uses those patterns to make predictions or generate new content — text, images, recommendations, decisions. It isn't "thinking" the way people do; it's pattern-matching at a massive scale. That distinction matters, because it explains both what AI is remarkably good at, and where it quietly gets things wrong.

A few quick facts

1950s

The term "Artificial Intelligence" was coined, long before the technology could do much at all.

Everywhere

Spam filters, maps, voice assistants, and autocomplete are all AI you likely use daily.

Not magic

AI systems are only as good — and as fair — as the data they were trained on.
